For teachers

Teams, roads and progress

Configure a shared hunt without leaving a learner or a road unassigned.

How team play is organised

Treasure Hunt can run for individuals or Moodle teams. In team mode, Moodle groups hold the students and groupings connect groups to a road. A road is the route that a team follows; it may differ from another road in stages, location or difficulty. Team progress is shared by the participants in that team.

Set up groups and groupings

  1. In the Moodle course, create the groups that will play and add participants to each group.
  2. Create groupings that contain the relevant groups. Give them names that match the planned roads.
  3. Set the Treasure Hunt activity to team mode.
  4. Create or validate each road, then assign its grouping. Check that every required road has one.
  5. Open the activity as a teacher and read the group warning card. Resolve students outside all relevant groups before the activity starts.
Two separate checks matter. A student without a suitable group cannot play. A road without an assigned grouping is also an error for team mode. The teacher dashboard surfaces both conditions.

Track and support teams

The activity progress list is organised by road and participant sequence. Review attempts, completion and scores to see who needs help. If itineraries are enabled, teachers can examine recorded movements while the game screen is open. Use the road or sequence clear action only when the corresponding run truly needs to be reset.

Designing several roads

Multiple roads can distribute teams across an area, reduce queues at QR points or offer comparable variants. Keep the educational goals equivalent and preview each road separately. The stage editor can copy stages from another road; review copied clues and geometry so the new road remains coherent.
